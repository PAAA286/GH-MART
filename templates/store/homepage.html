About Kwame Mensah

Kwame Mensah is a visionary entrepreneur with over 10 years of experience in the e-commerce industry. Born and raised in Accra, Ghana, Kwame founded GH MART with a mission to revolutionize online shopping in West Africa.

With a background in computer science and business administration from the University of Ghana, Kwame combines technical expertise with business acumen to drive innovation in the e-commerce space.

His passion for technology and commitment to excellence has positioned GH MART as Ghana's premier online shopping destination, serving thousands of satisfied customers across the country.
